Traditional Puerto Rican Style Pernil

This classic homemade pernil features a tender, flavorful pork seasoned to perfection. It's a simple yet satisfying dish that's perfect for any occasion.

It is the ultimate comfort meal for family dinners and gatherings. Serve it warm with a side of rice and beans or a crisp green salad.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cumin

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Season the pork shoulder with garlic powder, paprika, and cumin.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Seared the pork shoulder for 4-5 minutes on each side, or until desired doneness is reached.
  5. Let the pork rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
