Traditional Italian Lasagna

This classic homemade lasagna features rich layers of seasoned ground beef, savory marinara sauce, and tender pasta sheets. Each slice is packed with a creamy blend of ricotta, mozzarella, and parmesan cheese baked to perfection.

It is the ultimate comfort meal for family dinners and gatherings. Serve it warm with a side of garlic bread or a crisp green salad.

Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta, mozzarella, and parmesan cheeses.
  6. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture in a baking dish.
  7. Bake for 30-35 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
